🌾 AgriLink

AgriLink is a digital agricultural marketplace designed to strengthen market linkages and price discovery for farmers by connecting farmers and buyers on a unified platform.

The platform supports the complete agricultural trading workflow:

Produce Listing → Buyer Discovery → Offers → Counter-Offers → Transaction → Logistics

🎯 Problem

Farmers can face difficulties in finding reliable buyers, accessing transparent price information, negotiating effectively, and coordinating transactions and logistics. Small farmers may also struggle to meet the quantity requirements of larger buyers.

💡 Solution

AgriLink provides a centralized platform where:

- 👨‍🌾 Farmers can list their agricultural produce.
- 🛒 Buyers can discover available produce and make offers.
- 🤝 Farmers and buyers can negotiate through offers and counter-offers.
- 📦 Transactions and logistics can be digitally coordinated.

🛠️ Tech Stack

- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Backend: Node.js, Express.js
- Database: MySQL
- Architecture: REST API
- Security: Role-Based Authentication & Access Control

📌 Core Modules

- Produce Management
- Farmer Management
- Buyer Management
- Offers & Counter-Offers
- Transactions
- Logistics Tracking
- Analytics

🚀 Vision

To build a more transparent, connected, efficient, and farmer-centric agricultural trading ecosystem by reducing information gaps between farmers and the market.
